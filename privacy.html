Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026 · Effective: July 1, 2026

This policy explains what SignDeployer ("we," "us," "our") collects, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have — with specific attention to the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (together, the "CCPA/CPRA"). The short version: we collect only what's needed to run a paid sign-placement tool — your email, your subscription status, and the listings you choose to save — we do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Your California privacy choices

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know, delete, and correct the personal information we hold, to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. You will never be treated differently for exercising these rights.

We do not sell or share your personal information (including for cross-context behavioral advertising), so there is nothing to opt out of on that front. You can still control optional cookies and analytics at any time:

We also honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal: if your browser sends it, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of sharing and keep optional analytics off.

SignDeployer is a sign-placement tool for real-estate professionals, operated from California and available at signdeployer.com. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@signdeployer.com.

3. Sensitive personal information

We do not collect or process sensitive personal information (such as government identifiers, precise geolocation of your device, race, religion, health, sexual orientation, or account log-in credentials combined with access codes) for the purpose of inferring characteristics about you. Because we don't use it beyond what is necessary to provide the Service, the "right to limit" has nothing to restrict — but you can still contact us with any concern.

4. Sources of the information

We collect the information above directly from you (when you sign in, buy a plan, run a search, or save a listing), automatically from your device (basic request data and, only with your consent, anonymous usage measurements), and from our service providers (for example, subscription status from Stripe).

6. We do not sell or share your personal information

We have not sold personal information and have not shared it for cross-context behavioral advertising in the preceding 12 months, and we have no plans to. We do not use third-party advertising networks or ad-tech trackers. We also do not sell or share the personal information of minors under 16.

7. How we use the information

  • Provide, secure, and operate the Service (sign-in, computing sign plans, saving your listings).
  • Process payments and manage your subscription.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse (e.g., demo rate-limiting).
  • Respond to your requests and provide support.
  • Comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • With your consent, measure and improve performance.

8. Cookies, local storage, and tracking

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies (including your browser's local storage). They fall into three groups, and you control the optional ones:

  • Strictly necessary — keep you signed in (Supabase session), secure forms, and remember your privacy choices. These are always on because the site can't work without them.
  • Functional (optional) — remember your recent searches, settings, and a local cache of results so repeat searches are instant. Stored only in your browser.
  • Analytics (optional) — let us and Mapbox collect anonymous usage/performance measurements. Off unless you turn them on.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party ad networks. Manage your choices any time with the Your Privacy Choices link in the footer of every page, or here:

Clearing your browser storage removes local caches and your saved consent choice; saved listings remain in your account.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

Depending on where you live (California and several other states grant similar rights), you may have the right to:

  • Know / access the personal information we have collected about you, and how we've used and disclosed it.
  • Delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we don't do either).
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information (we don't use it beyond what's necessary).
  • Non-discrimination — we will not deny service, charge different prices, or provide a different quality of service because you exercised a right.

How to submit a request

Email hello@signdeployer.com with the right you want to exercise — including full deletion of your profile and saved listings, which we complete on request. You can cancel billing yourself any time from Account → Manage billing.

Verification & timing

To protect your data, we verify requests using the email address associated with your account (for account holders, we may send a confirmation to that email). We aim to confirm receipt within 10 business days and to respond within 45 days, extendable by a further 45 days where permitted, and we'll tell you if we need the extra time.

Authorized agents

You may use an authorized agent to submit a request. We may ask the agent for proof of your written permission and may still ask you to verify your identity directly.

Appeals

If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision or emailing hello@signdeployer.com with "Privacy Appeal" in the subject. If you are a California resident and have concerns, you may also contact the California Privacy Protection Agency or the California Attorney General.

10. California "Shine the Light"

California Civil Code § 1798.83 lets California residents ask about personal information shared with third parties for their direct-marketing purposes. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing, so no such disclosures are made.

11. Where your data lives

Account data and saved listings are stored with Supabase; the site is hosted on Netlify; payments are processed by Stripe; address searches are processed by Mapbox; sign-in emails are delivered via our email provider. Each processor maintains its own security and privacy program.

12. Security

Sign-in uses one-time email links (no passwords to leak). Access to paid features is verified server-side on every request. Database access is restricted with row-level security so each user can only read their own records. Secrets and API keys are stored in server environment variables, not in the site's code. The site is served over HTTPS with strict transport security.

13. How long we keep data

Account data is kept while your account exists. Saved listings are kept until you delete them. Demo rate-limit hashes are short-lived operational records. Billing records are retained as required for tax and accounting purposes. When you delete your account, we remove your profile and saved listings, except where we're required to keep certain records by law.

14. Children

The Service is for working real-estate professionals and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16, and we do not sell or share it.

15. Changes

If we materially change this policy, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and — where required — re-prompt you for consent.

16. Contact
